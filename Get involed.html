Volunteer Opportunities

We welcome community-minded individuals looking to contribute their time and skills to our cause.

Current Volunteer Needs:

  • Companions for elderly participants
  • Assistance with transportation to medical appointments
  • Help with minor home repairs and gardening
  • Workshop facilitators for various skills
  • Administrative support
  • Event coordination assistance

To apply as a volunteer, please fill out our volunteer application form or contact us directly.

Make a Donation

Your financial support helps us continue our vital programs and expand our reach in the community.

Ways to Donate:

  • One-time financial donation
  • Monthly sustaining donation
  • Sponsor a specific program
  • In-kind donations of goods or services

All donations are tax-deductible, and we provide receipts for your records.
