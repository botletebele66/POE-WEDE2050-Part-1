Our Programs

Discover our comprehensive programs designed to support and empower our community members.

Golden Years - Senior Program

Our Golden Years program provides a safe, engaging space for seniors to combat loneliness and maintain an active lifestyle.

Services Include

+
  • Social Activities: Group games, arts and crafts, and community gatherings
  • Gentle Exercise Classes: Yoga, stretching, and chair exercises
  • Shared Meals: Nutritious lunches and social dining experiences
  • Medication Management: Assistance with organizing and remembering medications
  • Transportation: Help getting to medical appointments
  • Health Monitoring: Regular blood pressure checks and wellness assessments

Schedule & Participation

+

Program Hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Drop-in Welcome: No appointment necessary

Cost: Free for all participants

Age Requirement: 60 years and older

Safe Harbour - Abuse Survivor Support

Our Safe Harbour program provides comprehensive support for survivors of domestic violence and elder abuse.

Services Include

+
  • 24/7 Crisis Intervention: Immediate support when you need it most
  • Confidential Counseling: One-on-one sessions with trained professionals
  • Safety Planning: Creating personalized plans for your protection
  • Legal Assistance: Referrals to legal aid and court support
  • Support Groups: Connect with others who understand your journey
  • Empowerment Workshops: Building confidence and life skills
  • Temporary Safe Accommodation: Referrals to safe shelters when needed

How to Access Support

+

Crisis Line: 0800 786 911 (Available 24/7)

Walk-in Hours: Monday-Sunday, 24 Hours

Confidentiality: All services are completely confidential

No Cost: All services are free of charge

You are not alone. We believe you, we support you, and we're here to help you heal.

Pathfinders - Life Skills Program

Our Pathfinders program helps individuals develop the skills needed to regain independence and find focus in life.

Program Components

+
  • Job Readiness Training: Resume writing, interview skills, and workplace etiquette
  • Financial Literacy: Budgeting, saving, and managing debt
  • Computer Skills: Basic to intermediate computer and internet training
  • Life Skills Workshops: Cooking, home maintenance, and personal care
  • Personal Development: Goal setting, time management, and communication
  • Mentorship Program: One-on-one guidance from community mentors
  • Certificate Programs: Recognized qualifications in various skills

Program Details

+

Duration: 6-week intensive program with ongoing support

Schedule: Flexible options - morning, afternoon, or evening classes

Requirements: Commitment to attend all sessions

Cost: Free for all participants

Materials: All learning materials provided

Next Intake: Programs start monthly - contact us to enroll
