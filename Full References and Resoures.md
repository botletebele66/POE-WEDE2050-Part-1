- **License**: MIT License

### Leaflet.js
- **Version**: 1.7.1
- **Source**: https://unpkg.com/leaflet@1.7.1/dist/leaflet.js
- **CSS**: https://unpkg.com/leaflet@1.7.1/dist/leaflet.css
